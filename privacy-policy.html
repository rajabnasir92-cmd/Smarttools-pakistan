Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This page explains privacy practices for visitors to SmartTools Pakistan.

Information entered into tools

The current tools are designed to work in your web browser. Text entered into a tool is processed in the browser to prepare or display a result. Do not enter passwords, financial details, identity numbers, or other sensitive information.

The website currently has no visitor account or sign-up system and does not operate a website database to save CVs or other text entered into these tools. Your own browser or device may retain information according to its settings.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting and internet services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request details. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Cookies and advertising

At the time of this update, this policy does not represent that Google AdSense ads are active on this website. If advertising or analytics services are added later, this policy must be updated to explain their use of cookies or similar technologies, and any required consent or disclosures must be provided.

External links

Third-party websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices. Please review their policies.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website changes. For questions, use our Contact Us page.
