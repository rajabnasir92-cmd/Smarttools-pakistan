A CV (Curriculum Vitae) is a document that presents your education, skills, experience and achievements. Employers use it to understand whether your background matches a role.

Why a good CV matters

  • It helps an employer understand your background quickly.
  • It presents your skills and achievements in an organized way.
  • It gives you a chance to show why you may be suitable for a role.

How to create a CV

  • Add your name and professional contact details.
  • Write a short career objective or profile relevant to the role.
  • List your education, including institutions and dates.
  • Add skills that are relevant and that you can honestly demonstrate.
  • Include work experience, internships, volunteer work or projects where relevant.
  • Mention useful certificates, awards or achievements.
  • Review spelling, dates and contact details before sharing.

Tips for students and beginners

  • If you do not have work experience, highlight academic projects, coursework, volunteering and practical skills.
  • Use clear headings and a simple layout.
  • Adjust your CV to the role you are applying for.
  • Be truthful; do not invent qualifications or experience.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Typos and incorrect contact details.
  • Unnecessary information unrelated to the role.
  • Hard-to-read fonts or crowded formatting.
  • Untrue claims or exaggerated experience.
