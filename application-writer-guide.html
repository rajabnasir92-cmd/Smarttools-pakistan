Application Writer Guide

Helpful learning guides from SmartTools Pakistan

A formal application is a written request sent to a school, office, organization or authority. A good application explains its purpose clearly and respectfully.

Basic parts of an application

  • Date, where required.
  • Name or title of the recipient, if known.
  • A clear subject line.
  • A respectful greeting.
  • A short explanation of your request and any necessary details.
  • A polite closing and your name.

How to write one

  • Identify exactly what you are requesting.
  • Use a clear and suitable subject line.
  • Explain the situation briefly and accurately.
  • Include dates or supporting details when relevant.
  • Use polite language and check the final draft.

Writing tips

  • Keep the message focused on one main request.
  • Use simple, formal language.
  • Be respectful without making the message unnecessarily long.
  • Check names, dates and other facts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Leaving the purpose unclear.
  • Adding unrelated personal details.
  • Using rude or overly casual language.
  • Submitting a draft without checking it.
