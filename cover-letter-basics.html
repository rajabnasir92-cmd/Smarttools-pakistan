Cover Letter Basics: A Simple Guide for Job Applications

Learn how to write a focused cover letter that introduces you and explains why you are applying.

Use a suitable greeting

If you know the recipient's name, use it accurately. Otherwise, “Dear Hiring Manager” is a reasonable general greeting.

Mention the role

State the job title and, when available, where you saw the vacancy. This helps the reader understand the purpose immediately.

Connect your skills to the role

Choose one or two relevant skills and briefly explain how you have used them in study, a project, volunteering or work.

Keep the tone professional

Use polite, direct language. Avoid overly familiar wording, unsupported claims and copied paragraphs that do not describe you.

Close courteously

Thank the reader for considering your application and say that you would welcome an opportunity to discuss your suitability.

Review the details

Check the employer's name, job title and your contact details before sending. A letter addressed to the wrong company can create a poor impression.

Remember: A cover letter should add context to your CV rather than repeat every line of it. Keep it relevant to the specific position and make sure every claim is accurate.
