A bio is a short description that tells people who you are, what you do and what information is relevant to the audience. It may be used for a profile, portfolio, school activity or professional page.

What makes a good bio?

  • It gives accurate information about you.
  • It is written for a particular audience and purpose.
  • It highlights a few relevant interests, skills or achievements.
  • It is short and easy to understand.

How to write a bio

  • Decide where the bio will appear and who will read it.
  • Start with your name or the role you want to describe.
  • Mention relevant studies, work, interests or skills.
  • Add one useful achievement or goal if appropriate.
  • Read it aloud and remove unnecessary words.

Different bio styles

  • Professional: focus on role, skills and relevant experience.
  • Student: mention your studies, interests and learning goals.
  • Social profile: keep it brief and share only details you are comfortable making public.

Privacy and accuracy tips

  • Do not include private information such as passwords, identity numbers or a home address.
  • Only share contact details when necessary and safe.
  • Do not invent qualifications or achievements.
