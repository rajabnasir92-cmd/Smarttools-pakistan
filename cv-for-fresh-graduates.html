How to Make a CV as a Fresh Graduate in Pakistan

A practical guide to preparing a clear, honest CV when you have little or no work experience.

Start with clear contact details

Add your full name, a professional email address, phone number and city. Do not include private details such as passwords, identity-document numbers or your full home address.

Write a short profile

Use two or three sentences to describe your education, strengths and the kind of opportunity you are seeking. Keep it specific and truthful.

Show your education clearly

List your latest qualification first. Include the institution, qualification and completion year or expected completion date.

Include relevant skills

Choose skills you can actually demonstrate, such as written communication, teamwork, basic spreadsheets or a language you use confidently.

Add projects and volunteering

School projects, community work, internships and volunteer activities can show useful experience. Explain your contribution instead of exaggerating your role.

Proofread before sending

Check spelling, dates, phone number and email address. Save a PDF copy and use a clear file name such as YourName-CV.pdf.

Remember: A good first CV does not need to be long. One clear page is often enough for a student or fresh graduate. Adjust it for each opportunity and never add experience or qualifications you do not have.
