How to Write a Short Professional Bio

A useful structure for a student, freelancer or early-career professional bio.

Say who you are

Start with your name and current role, field of study or area of interest.

Describe your focus

Mention the subjects, services or kinds of projects you work on. Be clear rather than trying to sound impressive.

Choose two strengths

Include strengths that are relevant and genuine. If possible, support them with a project, course or example.

Mention a meaningful goal

You can briefly explain what you are learning or the type of work you hope to contribute to.

Keep it appropriate for the platform

A short social profile may need one or two sentences. A portfolio bio can be a little longer and include selected projects.

Check accuracy and privacy

Avoid claims you cannot support and do not publish private contact details or personal information you do not want public.

Remember: A professional bio should sound like a real person. Use clear language, keep it up to date and tailor it to the audience who will read it.
