How to Write a Clear Formal Application

A straightforward structure for school, college and workplace requests.

Write the recipient

Begin with the appropriate title or office, such as The Principal or The Manager. Use the correct recipient for your situation.

Add a clear subject

Summarize the request in a short subject line, for example “Request for a Bonafide Certificate”.

Use a respectful opening

A greeting such as “Respected Sir/Madam” is commonly used in formal applications. Follow the institution's preferred format if it has one.

Explain the reason

State who you are and what you are requesting. Include only the facts needed to understand the request.

Make the request politely

Clearly say what action or consideration you are seeking. Avoid demanding language.

End and sign

Close politely, then include your name, class or department where relevant, and the date.

Remember: Before submitting, confirm the recipient, dates and supporting details. Keep a copy for your records and do not include sensitive personal information unless the institution specifically requires it through a safe process.
