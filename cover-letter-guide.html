Cover Letter Guide

Helpful learning guides from SmartTools Pakistan

A cover letter is a short message sent with a CV to explain why you are applying and how your skills relate to a particular role.

What to include

  • Your name and contact details, if appropriate.
  • The job title and organization you are applying to.
  • A brief opening explaining your interest.
  • One or two relevant skills, experiences or examples.
  • A polite closing thanking the reader.

Simple writing steps

  • Read the job advertisement and note its main requirements.
  • Choose examples that genuinely show your relevant skills.
  • Write a clear opening that names the role.
  • Explain how your experience or learning connects to the role.
  • Close politely and check the spelling and organization name.

Helpful tips

  • Keep the letter concise and specific to the role.
  • Use a professional, respectful tone.
  • Avoid copying the CV word for word.
  • Do not claim skills or experience you do not have.

Common mistakes

  • Sending the same generic letter for every role.
  • Using the wrong company or job title.
  • Writing very long paragraphs.
  • Forgetting to proofread.
